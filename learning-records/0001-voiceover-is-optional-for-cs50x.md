# CS50x allows a video without voiceover — but other CS50 courses do not

Sabra challenged the claim that narration was optional. Checked against the source:
CS50x says the video should "demonstrate your project in action, as with slides,
screenshots, voiceover, and/or live action" — the "and/or" makes a silent, captioned
video acceptable. The Cybersecurity course, by contrast, says you "minimally need to
include a voiceover", and the on-campus College version adds "ideally with you
yourself appearing in the video."

Two implications for future sessions. First, requirements differ per CS50 course, so
always read the page for *this* course rather than recalling a general CS50 rule.
Second, Sabra verifies claims rather than accepting them — lessons should carry
verbatim quotes and links, not paraphrase. Recorded in [[NOTES.md]].
