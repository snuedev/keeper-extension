# Mission: Screencast presentation for a CS50x final project

## Why
Sabra needs to submit Keeper (a Chrome extension for private notes) as their CS50x
final project, and CS50 requires a video of no more than 3 minutes presenting the
project. The video is the only part of the submission a human actually watches, so
it is what the project will be judged on. Recording is happening within a few days.

## Success looks like
- A video under 3:00 that opens with title, name, GitHub + edX usernames, city, country, and recording date
- A demo that shows Keeper actually working, not slides describing it
- At least one design decision explained out loud (the draft rescue is the strongest candidate)
- Uploaded to YouTube as unlisted or public (never private), URL pasted into README.md
- Sabra can do this again for a future project without re-learning it

## Constraints
- Recording within a few days: minimum viable beats polished
- Windows + WSL: the recorder must be a Windows application, WSL cannot capture a screen
- Junior developer, no prior screencasting or video editing experience
- No budget assumed: free tools only

## Out of scope
- Video editing beyond trimming (no transitions, music, motion graphics)
- Appearing on camera (optional for CS50x)
- Anything about the Chrome Web Store listing
